# Hand-Drawn / Organic Illustration / Human Mark

**Visible human hand, wobbly lines, watercolor bleed, ink splatter. Declaration of human authorship.**

## Visual DNA
Visible human hand as counter-signal to AI-generated perfection. Wobbly lines, imperfect shapes, visible brush strokes. Not childish — sophisticated illustration with intentional imperfection. Declaration of human authorship.

## Design Principles
1. **THE HAND IS THE SIGNAL** — Visible imperfection proves human authorship
2. **MEDIA AS TEXTURE** — Pencil grain, watercolor bleed, ink splatter
3. **ORGANIC FLOW** — Layout follows illustration, not grid

## Typography
- Handwritten or humanist fonts for display (Caveat, Patrick Hand)
- Clean sans for body
- Sometimes type is also hand-drawn

## Color
- Warm, earthy (terracotta, ochre, sage, cream, charcoal)
- Or bold marker colors (cobalt, vermillion, lemon)

## Motion
- Gentle, organic
- SVG draw-on animations (stroke-dasharray)
- Wobble/breathing effects
- Nothing mechanical

## Layout
- Loose, organic
- Illustrations break out of containers
- Asymmetric with hand-drawn dividers
- Marginalia and sketch notes as design elements

## Texture
- Visible media texture — pencil grain, watercolor bleed, ink splatter, marker streaks, eraser marks
- Paper texture underneath

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `hand-drawn`, `illustration`, `watercolor bleed`, `ink splatter`, `human mark`, `bespoke`
**Banned:** `AI-generated imagery`, `perfect geometric shapes`, `corporate polish`, `digital precision`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Hand-drawn or illustrated elements
- [ ] Visible media texture (pencil, watercolor, ink)
- [ ] Organic, imperfect shapes
- [ ] Warm, earthy or bold marker colors
- [ ] Gentle, organic motion
- [ ] Layout follows illustration
- [ ] No AI-generated or perfect geometric imagery
- [ ] No corporate polish or digital precision

## When to Use
Education/edtech, nonprofit/NGO, wellness/mental health, children's brands, independent publishers, artisan food
