# When to Use

## Perfect For

| Context | Why This Style Works |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Education/edtech** | Sketchy, friendly illustration makes learning feel approachable and human |
| **Nonprofits/NGOs** | The handmade, sincere aesthetic signals authenticity and human mission |
| **Wellness/mental health** | Warm, imperfect marks feel reassuring — the opposite of clinical |
| **Children's brands** | Doodles, wobble motion, and marker colors are native to childhood |
| **Independent publishers** | Hand-drawn covers and marginalia give small press an artisanal identity |
| **Artisan food** | Watercolor bleed, paper texture, and ink labels echo craft and provenance |
| **Illustrator/designer portfolios** | The style *is* the medium — showcasing illustration as the product |
| **Craft & stationery brands** | Marker texture, sketch notes, and paper grain match the physical goods |

## Brand Personality Match

This style reinforces brands that are:
- **Human** — visible hand, visible imperfection, a declaration of human authorship
- **Warm** — earthy palette (terracotta, ochre, sage, cream, charcoal), never cold
- **Artisanal** — bespoke, crafted, one-of-one rather than mass-produced
- **Imperfect** — comfortable with wobble, bleed, and splatter; nothing mechanical
- **Bespoke** — every page feels individually drawn, not templated

## Avoid When

| Context | Why It Fails |
|---------|-------------|
| **Corporate/B2B services** | Hand-drawn informality undercuts gravitas and perceived scale |
| **Finance/banking** | Trust in this category reads as precision — sketchiness signals unreliability |
| **Legal/government** | Regulatory and compliance content needs neutral, serious presentation |
| **Enterprise SaaS (technical)** | Complex dense UIs need grid discipline the organic layout can't provide |
| **Data-heavy dashboards** | Loose layout and wobble make numbers and charts feel imprecise |
| **Photography-led brands** | The style competes with photography instead of supporting it |
| **Luxury/high-end products** | Heritage luxury wants polish and restraint, not hand-drawn charm |

## How to Decide

Ask: **"Does my brand want to declare a human hand — warm, artisanal, visibly imperfect?"**

If yes → Hand-Drawn / Organic Illustration / Human Mark.
If no → Consider Playful Interactive Maximal (if you want playfulness with loud energy), Organic Botanical Warmth (if you want warmth and organic shapes without the sketchy hand), or Bold Anti-Corporate Color (if you want to break corporate norms through color and layout, not illustration).
