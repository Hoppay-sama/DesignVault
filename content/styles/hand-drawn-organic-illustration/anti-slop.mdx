# Anti-Slop Checklist

- [ ] Hand-drawn or illustrated elements throughout (not just one hero image)
- [ ] Visible media texture — pencil grain, watercolor bleed, ink splatter, marker streaks, eraser marks
- [ ] Paper/canvas texture underneath everything (cream `#F5F0E1` base)
- [ ] Organic, imperfect shapes — wobbly circles, uneven lines, intentional wobble
- [ ] Warm earthy palette (terracotta, ochre, sage, charcoal) or bold marker (cobalt, vermillion, lemon)
- [ ] Handwritten or sketch-style display type (Caveat, Patrick Hand) paired with clean sans body
- [ ] Gentle, organic motion — SVG draw-on (stroke-dasharray), wobble, breathing (not mechanical)
- [ ] Layout follows illustration — asymmetric, elements break out of containers
- [ ] Hand-drawn dividers, arrows, and marginalia/sketch notes as design elements
- [ ] Slight rotation on elements (1-3deg) — nothing perfectly level
- [ ] Imperfection is a feature — visible hand strokes, color bleeding outside the lines
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (static, no draw-on/wobble/breathing)
- [ ] No AI-generated or photorealistic imagery (this style declares human authorship)
- [ ] No perfect geometric shapes or sterile vector-perfect lines
- [ ] No corporate polish, digital precision, or ultra-clean flat UI
- [ ] No fake sketch filters or "aged paper" overlays slapped onto an otherwise corporate design
- [ ] No uniform 1px borders or straight horizontal rules as separators
- [ ] No perfectly centered, grid-locked compositions
- [ ] No flat, evenly-distributed color fills (color should look applied by hand)
- [ ] No mechanical spring/tween motion or snappy transitions
