## ANIMATIONS

- Entrance: opacity 0→1, 200ms, ease-out (staggered 40ms) — functional, not theatrical
- Hover: color shifts and underline reveals only, 150ms
- Tabular/number changes: immediate or 120ms crossfade — numbers don't animate for fun
- No parallax, no scroll-triggered reveals beyond a single fade

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → disable all transitions, instant states.

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** 4-8 words. Direct, objective, system-voiced. "Precision Instruments for Engineers" not "Discover the Future of Tools."

**Body text:** 2-3 sentences. Factual, spec-like. Write like a technical document, not a marketing pitch.

**Annotations:** Mono labels everywhere — `NO. 01`, `FIG. A`, `Q3 / REV. 2`, coordinates. The page should be understandable as a drawing.

**Numbers:** Tabular figures, right-aligned in columns, ISO-style formatting. Numbers are content, not decoration.
