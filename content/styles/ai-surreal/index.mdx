# AI-Surreal / Latent Space

**Dreamlike synthetic imagery, desaturated pastels with saturated accents, morphing transitions, parallax through latent space.**

## Visual DNA
The uncanny beauty of machine-generated imagery embraced as aesthetic. Dreamlike compositions where scale, material, and physics don't obey reality. Desaturated pastels with sudden saturated accents. The design acknowledges its synthetic nature.

## Design Principles
1. **SYNTHETIC AS AESTHETIC** — AI imagery isn't hidden — it's the star
2. **IMAGE DOMINATES** — Full-bleed surreal compositions, minimal text
3. **DRIFT, NOT DANCE** — Slow dreamlike motion, morphing transitions

## Typography
- Clean sans-serif that doesn't compete (Geist, Inter, Söhne)
- Minimal — imagery carries all weight
- Small, floating placement

## Color
- Desaturated pastels with sudden saturated accents
- Dreamlike color grading
- Iridescent/oily surface colors

## Motion
- Slow dreamlike drifts
- Morphing transitions between states
- Parallax through latent space
- Subtle breathing/pulsing

## Layout
- Image-dominant, full-bleed surreal compositions
- Text overlays minimal and translucent
- Asymmetric, floating

## Texture
- AI-generated textures — impossible materials, hybrid surfaces
- Intentional artifacts as design elements

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `latent space`, `synthetic media`, `dreamlike`, `morphing transitions`, `desaturated pastels`
**Banned:** `realistic photography`, `geometric precision`, `corporate minimalism`, `sharp edges`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] AI/synthetic imagery as hero
- [ ] Desaturated pastel palette
- [ ] Morphing or dreamlike motion
- [ ] Minimal text over imagery
- [ ] Intentional surreal/uncanny elements
- [ ] No realistic stock photography
- [ ] No corporate or geometric precision

## When to Use
AI/ML companies, creative tech, experimental portfolios, art direction for tech brands, music visuals
