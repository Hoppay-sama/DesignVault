# Design Principles

## 1. SYNTHETIC AS AESTHETIC

AI-generated imagery isn't hidden or apologized for — it's the star. The uncanny beauty of machine-generated images is embraced as a legitimate aesthetic. Dreamlike compositions where scale, material, and physics don't obey reality. The design acknowledges its synthetic nature and celebrates it. This isn't "AI pretending to be photography" — it's AI imagery as its own art form.

**What it prevents:** The tendency to treat AI imagery as a compromise or to try to make it look "real." The synthetic quality IS the aesthetic — the artifacts, the morphing, the impossible materials.

---

## 2. IMAGE DOMINATES

Full-bleed surreal compositions fill the viewport. Text is minimal, floating, translucent. The image carries 90% of the visual weight. If you remove the text, the page should still feel complete — the imagery is the content. Text is metadata, annotation, a whisper over the visual roar.

**What it prevents:** Text-heavy layouts that treat imagery as decoration. In this style, the imagery IS the message — text just provides context.

---

## 3. DRIFT, NOT DANCE

Motion is slow, dreamlike, and morphing. Nothing snaps, nothing bounces, nothing moves with mechanical precision. Elements drift like objects in a dream — slowly, continuously, with morphing transitions between states. The motion should feel like watching a latent space interpolation — one image slowly becoming another.

**What it prevents:** Snappy, energetic motion that feels like a tech product. This style moves like a dream — slow, continuous, morphing.

---

## 4. DESATURATED PASTELS WITH SATURATED ACCENTS

The base palette is desaturated pastels — soft pinks, muted lavenders, dusty blues, pale greens. But within these muted fields, sudden saturated accents appear — a vivid magenta flower, an electric blue ripple, a saturated orange sphere. The contrast between the muted base and the saturated accent creates a dreamlike color dynamic — like a memory where most things are faded but one detail is vivid.

**What it prevents:** Uniformly saturated palettes (too digital) or uniformly desaturated palettes (too flat). The dreamlike quality comes from the contrast.

---

## 5. INTENTIONAL ARTIFACTS

AI imagery has artifacts — weird hands, impossible geometry, texture glitches. In this style, these aren't bugs — they're design elements. The uncanny valley is the aesthetic. Don't try to fix or hide artifacts; feature them. The artifacts are what make the imagery feel machine-imagined, not human-photographed.

**What it prevents:** Over-processing AI imagery to make it look "clean." The artifacts are the signature — they say "this was made by a machine, and that's the point."
