## ANIMATIONS

- Hero imagery: opacity 0→1 + scale(1.06)→1, 2500ms, ease-out
- Text: opacity 0→1 + translateY, 1200-1500ms, ease-out (staggered delays)
- All imagery: drift (scale + opacity breathing), 8-12s, infinite
- Morphing UI: border-radius morph, 12s, infinite
- Iridescent: gradient shift, 8s, infinite

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → disable all drift/morph/breathing, instant entrance.
