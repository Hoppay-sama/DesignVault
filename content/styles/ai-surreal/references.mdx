## Key Visual References

### AI Imagery Style
- **Impossible materials** — liquid metal flowers, glass clouds, stone water
- **Hybrid surfaces** — materials blending into each other
- **Scale ambiguity** — tiny galaxies, massive cells, landscapes inside drops
- **Intentional artifacts** — AI glitches featured as design elements

### Color References
- **Desaturated pastels** — dusty rose #E8D5D5, muted lavender #D5D5E8, dusty blue #D5DEE8
- **Saturated accents** — vivid magenta #FF006E, electric blue #3A86FF, deep violet #8338EC
- **Tinted black** — #0D0D12 (slight purple tint, not pure black)
- **Translucent text** — 85% opacity warm off-white

### Typography References
- **Geist Light** — neutral, invisible, doesn't compete with imagery
- **Inter Light (300)** — clean, readable, light weight
- **Small sizes** — display max 3rem, body 14-16px, micro 10-12px
- **Wide tracking on labels** — 0.15-0.25em, uppercase

### Motion References
- **Drift** — 8-12s, barely perceptible, ease-in-out, infinite
- **Morphing** — border-radius shifts, 12s, organic
- **Breathing** — 2% scale, 10% opacity, 6s, ease-in-out
- **Entrance** — 2500ms for imagery, 1500ms for text (VERY slow)

## Screenshot Notes

> When capturing references for this style, look for:
> - The imagery (must be AI-generated, not photographic)
> - The color palette (must be desaturated base with rare saturated accents)
> - The motion (must be slow, drift, morph — not snappy)
> - The text density (must be minimal — imagery carries 90% of weight)
> - The UI shapes (must be morphing, organic — not sharp/geometric)
> - The backgrounds (must be full-bleed imagery — no solid colors)
> - The overall mood (must feel dreamlike, synthetic, uncanny)
