# Quiet Editorial

**Generous whitespace, restrained typography, content that breathes. Silence as design. Longform readable.**

## Visual DNA
Silence as design. Generous whitespace, restrained typography, content that breathes. Page feels like well-edited publication — every word earns its place. Motion nearly absent. Design trusts reader to engage without spectacle.

## Design Principles
1. **DESIGN DISAPPEARS** — Interface recedes so only content remains
2. **READABILITY IS EVERYTHING** — 65ch max, 1.6+ line height
3. **MINIMAL MOTION, MAXIMAL TRUST** — Absence of animation signals confidence

## Typography
- Clean sans-serif or refined serif
- Moderate sizes (32-48px headlines)
- Generous leading
- Longform readable

## Color
- Near-monochrome (off-white, warm gray, near-black)
- Minimal accent
- No gradients

## Motion
- Minimal fade-in on scroll
- No parallax, no bouncy animations
- Instant or near-instant transitions

## Layout
- Single column or simple 2-column
- Generous margins (max-w-[65ch])
- Vertical rhythm
- No bento grids

## Texture
- Clean, paper-like
- No grain, no depth effects

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `generous whitespace`, `restrained typography`, `quiet confidence`, `longform readable`, `invisible design`
**Banned:** `decorative animations`, `busy layouts`, `loud colors`, `spectacle over substance`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Generous whitespace throughout
- [ ] Restrained typography (no bold/display extremes)
- [ ] Minimal motion (fade-in only)
- [ ] Longform readable (65ch max, 1.6+ line height)
- [ ] Near-monochrome palette
- [ ] No decorative animations
- [ ] No busy layouts or loud colors
- [ ] Content-forward, design-invisible

## When to Use
Research publications, policy content, thought leadership, documentation, portfolios (read.cv)

## Prompts
**Exact:** Build a quiet editorial page with generous whitespace, restrained typography, minimal motion, longform readability. Content-forward, design-invisible aesthetic.

**Template:** Build a [BRAND] quiet editorial page with vast whitespace, clean type, minimal motion, longform focus. Trust through restraint.
