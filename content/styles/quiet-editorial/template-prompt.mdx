- NO decorative animations (fade-in only, 600-900ms)
- NO busy layouts (single column, simple 2-column at most)
- NO loud colors (near-monochrome; one muted accent at most)
- NO spectacle over substance (content is the hero)
- NO parallax, no scroll effects
- NO bouncy animations (no springs, no overshoot)
- NO bento grids or card grids
- NO gradients (flat color only)
- NO grain or depth effects (clean, paper-like)
- NO bold/display extremes (moderate sizes, restrained weights)

## STRUCTURE

### Hero
- `min-h-[85dvh]`, centered, max-w-[65ch], generous padding
- Kicker: uppercase 11px, tracking 0.2em, gray
- Headline: [DISPLAY_FONT] 400, `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)`, sentence case

### Longform Article
- Single column, max-w-[65ch], space-y-8
- Drop cap on first paragraph (display serif, ~3em, float-left)
- Pull quote mid-article: [DISPLAY_FONT] italic, `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2rem)`

### Editorial Grid (simple 2-column)
- Essay links separated by hairline 1px rules — no cards, no backgrounds

### Footer
- Hairline top border, minimal, uppercase gray text

## ANIMATIONS

- Hero: opacity 0→1 + translateY(8px)→0, 700ms, ease-out
- Sections: fade-in on scroll, 700ms, ease-out
- Pull quote: fade + subtle rise, 800ms, ease-out
- Hairlines: opacity 0→1, 500ms, ease-out

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → instant entrance, no transitions.

---

## VISUAL RULES (DO NOT MODIFY)

- Generous whitespace — at least 40% empty space per viewport
- Restrained typography — no bold or display extremes
- Minimal motion — slow fades only, no springs, no parallax
- Longform readable — max-w-[65ch], 1.6+ line height
- Near-monochrome palette — one muted accent at most
- Clean paper-like texture — no grain, no depth effects
- Content-forward, design-invisible

---

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** 4-8 words. Quiet, declarative. "The quiet power of the well-set page" not "Revolutionizing Content With Our Platform."

**Body text:** Written for sustained reading. Editorial, measured tone. Essays, research, thought leadership — not marketing copy.

**Structure:** Longform columns with generous vertical rhythm. Drop caps, pull quotes, hairline rules. The words carry the page.
