# Organic Botanical Warmth

**Earth tones, botanical illustration, paper grain, warm serif, hand-drawn elements. Nature-forward, tactile, warm.**

## Visual DNA
Nature-forward, tactile, and warm. Real botanical photography, hand-drawn illustrations, earthy textures. Deep backgrounds with luminous botanical imagery that glows like bioluminescent specimens. Artisan craft over digital perfection.

## Design Principles
1. **NATURE AS MATERIAL** — Botanical photography, leaf venation, petal texture provide all visual richness
2. **EARTH TONE PALETTE** — Terracotta, sage, warm ochre, deep olive, clay
3. **WARMTH OVER POLISH** — Slight imperfection signals human craft

## Typography
- Display: Organic serifs (Recoleta, Cormorant, Playfair Display) or hand-drawn faces
- Body: Warm serif italic for emphasis, clean sans for body
- Size: Moderate (not monumental, not tiny)

## Color
- Ground: Deep forest #0A1A0F or midnight green #0D1B2A
- Accents: Emerald, sage, gold leaf, copper
- Muted jewel tones for florals

## Motion
- Gentle — leaf drift, soft parallax on nature photography
- SVG draw-on animations for illustrations (stroke-dasharray)
- Fade-in reveals like emerging from darkness

## Layout
- Generous, gallery-like
- Botanical illustrations as full-bleed elements or framed specimens
- Asymmetric with organic flow

## Texture
- Paper grain
- Watercolor wash
- Botanical illustration detail
- Gold foil stamping effect
- Dark paper or velvet surface quality

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `botanical illustration`, `earth tone palette`, `paper grain`, `watercolor wash`, `organic serif`, `gold foil`
**Banned:** `neon colors`, `geometric shapes`, `tech aesthetics`, `cold blue tones`, `minimalist sans-serif`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Earth tone palette (terracotta, sage, ochre)
- [ ] Botanical or nature imagery
- [ ] Paper grain or watercolor texture
- [ ] Warm serif typography
- [ ] Hand-drawn or organic elements
- [ ] Gold foil or copper accents
- [ ] No neon, geometric, or tech aesthetics

## When to Use
Luxury skincare/wellness, premium spirits/wine, boutique hospitality, high-end cannabis, botanical gardens, fine dining, perfume brands
