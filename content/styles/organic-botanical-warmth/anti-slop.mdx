# Anti-Slop Checklist

- [ ] Earth tone palette (terracotta, sage, ochre, olive, clay)
- [ ] Deep forest background (#0A1A0F) or dark earth (#1A1410) — not pure black
- [ ] Botanical photography or hand-drawn illustrations
- [ ] Paper grain overlay (0.02-0.04 opacity)
- [ ] Watercolor wash backgrounds (subtle, blurred)
- [ ] Warm organic serif typography (Cormorant, Recoleta, Playfair)
- [ ] Italic emphasis on key words with gold color
- [ ] Gold foil effect on accents (gradient with shimmer, not flat #FFD700)
- [ ] Warm cream text (#F5F0E8) on dark backgrounds — not pure white
- [ ] Hand-drawn botanical illustrations with labels (FOLIUM, RADIX, FLOS)
- [ ] SVG draw-on animations for illustrations (stroke-dasharray)
- [ ] Gentle motion (800ms+ durations, ease-out, no bounce)
- [ ] Gold-bordered CTAs with earth-tone fill on hover
- [ ] No neon or saturated synthetic colors
- [ ] No geometric shapes or tech aesthetics
- [ ] No cold blue tones
- [ ] No minimalist sans-serif as primary typeface
- [ ] No flat design — everything tactile and textured
- [ ] No pure black (#000000) background
- [ ] No pure white (#FFFFFF) text on dark
- [ ] No digital gradients — use watercolor wash
- [ ] No leaf icons — use real botanical photography/illustration
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (instant reveals, no drift)
