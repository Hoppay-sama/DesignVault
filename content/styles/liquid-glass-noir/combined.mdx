# Motion, Layout, Texture, Vocabulary, Anti-Slop, When-to-Use, Mistakes & References

> **Note:** This style's motion, layout, and texture sections are tightly coupled with the glass effect. Key specs below.

## Motion

| Element | Animation | Duration | Easing |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Glass panels entrance | Opacity + translateY(16px)→0 + scale(0.98)→1 | 800ms | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` |
| Hover lift | translateY(-4px) + subtle scale(1.01) | 300ms | Spring (stiffness: 300, damping: 25) |
| Video fade-in | Opacity 0→1 | 1500ms | `ease-out` |
| Stagger between panels | — | — | 0.08s delay |

**Spring physics for hover is essential.** Linear hover feels cheap on glass.

## Layout

- **Z-depth stacking:** Glass panels at different Z-levels create depth
- **Floating navigation:** Nav bar floats with glass treatment, detached from top edge
- **Card grid:** Bento-style with glass cells, 1px gap
- **Hero:** Video full-bleed behind, glass panels floating above

## Texture

- **Backdrop-filter blur:** 12-24px on glass surfaces
- **Edge refraction:** Liquid-glass border gradient (see Color section)
- **Saturation boost:** `saturate(1.2)` in backdrop-filter for vibrancy through glass
- **Video:** Slow, abstract, dark ambient footage

## Vocabulary

**Primary:** `backdrop-filter blur`, `frosted surface`, `edge refraction`, `liquid-glass border`, `Z-depth stacking`, `floating glass`
**Secondary:** `glass pane layering`, `inner border highlight`, `spring physics hover`, `translucent panel`, `video atmosphere`, `saturation boost`
**Banned:** `opaque panels`, `heavy shadows`, `solid backgrounds`, `multiple glass tints`, `pure black glass`

## Anti-Slop

- [ ] No opaque glass backgrounds (must be translucent)
- [ ] No backdrop-blur > 40px (washes out content)
- [ ] No drop shadows on glass (border does the work)
- [ ] No more than 3 glass layers at same blur level
- [ ] No colored glass tinting (keep neutral)
- [ ] Video must be slow/abstract (not distracting)
- [ ] Text on glass must pass WCAG contrast
- [ ] Spring physics on hover (not linear)
- [ ] Liquid-glass border gradient present
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored

## When to Use

**Perfect for:** Premium SaaS, AI/ML products, Apple-adjacent brands, luxury tech, fintech, spatial computing interfaces
**Avoid when:** Content-heavy sites, editorial, accessibility-critical (glass reduces readability), low-performance targets (backdrop-filter is expensive)

## Mistakes

1. **Too much blur** → Content behind glass becomes unreadable. Fix: 12-24px max.
2. **No edge refraction** → Glass looks like a flat translucent rectangle. Fix: liquid-glass border gradient.
3. **Video too busy** → Distracts from content. Fix: slow, abstract, dark footage.
4. **All surfaces glass** → No hierarchy. Fix: mix glass with solid near-black.
5. **Poor text contrast** → White text on frosted glass over bright video areas fails. Fix: test contrast, add text shadow if needed.
