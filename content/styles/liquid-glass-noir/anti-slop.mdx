# Anti-Slop Checklist

- [ ] Dark cinematic video backgrounds (0.85 opacity over `#0A0A0A`) — never light or colorful backdrops
- [ ] Near-transparent glass (`rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`) — the blur does the work, not the background color
- [ ] Backdrop blur 12-24px, varied per Z-depth (back 12 / mid 20 / front 24)
- [ ] Liquid-glass border (inset gradient + mask-composite) on every panel
- [ ] One consistent light direction across all panels (highlight streaks aligned)
- [ ] Subtle iridescent highlights (white → blue 100,180,255, 10-15% opacity)
- [ ] Floating glass layers with slow fluid motion (7-16s loops, spring hover)
- [ ] Liquid reveals — mask wipe + blur-to-sharp, never slide-ins
- [ ] Clean geometric sans at medium weight (Inter Tight, Satoshi, SF Pro Display, Geist)
- [ ] White text with verified contrast against frosted surfaces
- [ ] Asymmetric, Z-stacked layouts — panels offset, never a symmetric grid
- [ ] Panels separated by dark void and soft shadows (no dividers, no borders between)
- [ ] Noir restraint — dark, moody, cinematic; no purple, no cream
- [ ] No default frosted-card template look (identical rounded cards in a grid)
- [ ] No flat white-glass clichés (solid white gradients, no refraction, no light logic)
- [ ] No heavy backdrop-blur (>40px) that destroys readability
- [ ] No colored glass tinting — glass stays neutral/clear
- [ ] No opaque panels
- [ ] No uniform blur across layers (collapses the depth illusion)
- [ ] No fast or bouncy motion (glass is heavy; 300ms+ minimum)
- [ ] No bright backgrounds — the video must never outshine the glass
- [ ] No stock-photo-style light backgrounds or rainbow iridescence
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (static panels, video paused or poster frame)
