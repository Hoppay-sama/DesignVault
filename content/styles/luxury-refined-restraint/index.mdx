# Luxury Refined Restraint

**Extreme whitespace, refined serif, museum-quality photography, gallery spacing. Whispered confidence.**

## Visual DNA
Extreme restraint signals expense. Vast whitespace, precise typography, museum-quality photography. Every element considered — nothing accidental. Gallery-like spacing. Design disappears to let subject speak.

## Design Principles
1. **WHISPER, DON'T SHOUT** — Confidence without volume
2. **EVERY PIXEL CONSIDERED** — Nothing accidental
3. **GALLERY SPACING** — Elements separated like museum artworks

## Typography
- Refined serif or elegant sans (Canela, Domaine, Tiempos, Söhne)
- Generous leading
- Small body text

## Color
- Muted, desaturated — warm stone, cool silver, deep charcoal
- Occasional single rich accent (oxblood, emerald)

## Motion
- Minimal — slow crossfades, gentle image reveals
- No decorative motion

## Layout
- Extreme whitespace (py-48+)
- Single-column focus
- Centered or edge-aligned
- Gallery-like spacing

## Texture
- High-quality photography
- Subtle paper or stone texture
- No digital noise

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `museum whitespace`, `refined restraint`, `gallery spacing`, `luxury serif`, `whispered confidence`
**Banned:** `loud colors`, `busy layouts`, `decorative motion`, `cheap stock photography`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Extreme whitespace (py-48+ sections)
- [ ] Refined serif or elegant sans
- [ ] Museum-quality photography
- [ ] Minimal motion only
- [ ] Muted, desaturated palette
- [ ] Considered, deliberate placement
- [ ] No loud colors or busy layouts
- [ ] No decorative animations

## When to Use
Luxury fashion houses, high-end real estate, premium artisan brands, fine jewelry, haute couture
