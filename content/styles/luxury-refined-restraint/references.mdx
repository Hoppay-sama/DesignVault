## Key Visual References

### Photography Style
- **Museum-quality composition** — single subjects, soft natural light, deliberate negative space
- **Muted desaturated tones** — no saturated skies, no flashy backgrounds
- **Warm neutrals** — ivory, champagne, warm stone, cool silver, espresso
- **One rich accent** — oxblood #6E2530, emerald #1E4D3B, or deep gold #A8874E, used in details only

### Typography References
- **Cormorant Garamond 300/400** — high-contrast luxury serif, available on Google Fonts
- **Canela / Domaine** — premium licensed serifs, warm and editorial
- **Söhne** — premium neutral sans for body text
- **Small body text** — 12-14px, line-height 1.9, generous leading
- **Micro-labels** — 11-12px uppercase, tracking 0.25em
- **Display scale** — max 4rem, sentence case, never all-caps

### Color References
- **Warm ivory ground** — #F7F3EC
- **Champagne beige** — #EDE4D3
- **Warm stone** — #C9BFB0, **cool silver** — #A9AEAD
- **Espresso** — #2E2622, **deep charcoal** — #1E1B19
- **Muted text** — 55% opacity espresso on ivory

### Motion References
- **Crossfades** — 600-1000ms on scroll into view (slow, once)
- **Image reveals** — 1000ms, translateY(24px)→0, ease-out
- **Entrance** — 1200ms for imagery, 900ms for text (deliberate, unhurried)
- **No loops** — nothing animates infinitely; motion is a rare whisper

## Screenshot Notes

> When capturing references for this style, look for:
> - The whitespace (must be extreme — elements separated like museum artworks)
> - The photography (must be museum-quality, muted — never stock)
> - The palette (must be desaturated warm neutrals with at most one rich accent)
> - The typography (must be refined serif or elegant sans, small body, generous leading)
> - The motion (must be slow crossfades and gentle reveals — not snappy, no loops)
> - The layout (must be single-column, centered or edge-aligned, gallery spacing)
> - The overall mood (must feel expensive, considered, whispered — nothing accidental)
