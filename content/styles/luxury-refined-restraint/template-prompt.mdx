## MOTION

- Hero imagery: opacity 0→1 + scale(1.03)→1, 1200ms, ease-out
- Display text: opacity 0→1 + translateY(16px), 900ms, ease-out (400ms delay)
- Section crossfades: opacity 0→1 on scroll into view, 600-1000ms, ease-out
- Image reveals: opacity 0→1 + translateY(24px), 1000ms, ease-out, once per view

No infinite or looping animations. Nothing moves unless the user scrolls.

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → disable all animations, instant entrance.
