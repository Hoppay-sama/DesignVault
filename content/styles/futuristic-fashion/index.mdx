# Futuristic Fashion

**Pure white minimal futuristic fashion interface. Black text on white. Mouse-tracking spotlight reveal. Wireframe globe, checkerboard, L-corner brackets.**

## Visual DNA
Pure white minimal futuristic fashion. Black text on white. Mouse-tracking spotlight reveals a second image inside a soft circular mask on the dark background image. Wireframe globe SVG, checkerboard accents, L-corner brackets frame the composition. Side drawers for shop/collections/journal. Technical precision meets fashion editorial.

## Design Principles
1. **WHITE AS CANVAS** — Not cream, not off-white — pure white (#FFFFFF). Black ink only
2. **INTERACTIVE REVEAL** — Mouse spotlight (ease 0.1, radius clamp 160-420px) reveals hidden image layer
3. **TECHNICAL FRAMING** — L-corner brackets, wireframe globes, checkerboard patterns signal precision

## Typography
- Display: Orbitron 600-900 (geometric, futuristic), clamp(2rem, 4.5vw, 5.25rem)
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans 400-700, clamp(0.7rem, 0.35vw + 0.55rem, 0.9rem)

## Color
- Ground: #FFFFFF (pure white)
- Text: #000000 (pure black)
- Accents: gray-200 through gray-600 only
- Grid: slate #64748b at 10% opacity
- NO purple, NO cream, NO color

## Motion
- Mouse-tracking spotlight (ease factor 0.1, radius clamp(160px, 16vw, 420px))
- Parallax grid offset (ease factor 0.06)
- Side drawer slide-in (cubic-bezier(0.76, 0, 0.24, 1))

## Layout
- Full viewport, overflow-hidden
- Asymmetric: headline left, feature block right-bottom
- L-corner brackets framing content
- Side drawers (right slide-in) for navigation

## Texture
- Dual-image reveal background (base + spotlight-revealed layer)
- Subtle SVG grid overlay (10% opacity)
- Checkerboard pattern accent
- Wireframe globe SVG

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `pure white minimal`, `mouse spotlight reveal`, `wireframe globe`, `checkerboard accent`, `L-corner brackets`, `side drawer navigation`
**Banned:** `cream backgrounds`, `purple accents`, `centered layouts`, `card-based design`, `colorful imagery`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Pure white (#FFFFFF) ground
- [ ] Black text only
- [ ] Gray accents only (no color)
- [ ] Mouse-tracking spotlight reveal
- [ ] Wireframe globe or technical SVG decoration
- [ ] L-corner bracket framing
- [ ] Side drawer navigation
- [ ] Fluid clamp sizing throughout
- [ ] No purple, cream, or colored accents

## When to Use
Fashion brands, luxury product launches, design studios, architectural firms, premium product pages
