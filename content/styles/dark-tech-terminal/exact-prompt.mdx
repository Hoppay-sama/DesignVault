```css

  background: repeating-linear-gradient(
    to bottom,
    transparent 0px,

    rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25) 3px

  text-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.7);

| Element | Animation | Duration | Easing |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|

| Caret | opacity 1→0 blink | 1s | `steps(1)`, infinite |
