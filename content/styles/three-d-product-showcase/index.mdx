# 3D Product Showcase / Object Theater

**Photorealistic 3D renders, orbital rotation, studio lighting, scroll-driven camera movements, exploded views.**

## Visual DNA
Products as sculptural objects in a virtual studio. Real-time 3D rendering replaces photography. Users orbit, zoom, interact with photorealistic models. Studio lighting with soft key light, subtle fill, controlled rim light.

## Design Principles
1. **PRODUCT AS SCULPTURE** — 3D model art-directed like a photography shoot
2. **INTERACTION IS EXPLORATION** — Scroll-driven camera movements
3. **STUDIO LIGHTING** — Soft key, subtle fill, controlled rim

## Typography
- Minimal, functional
- Product specs in mono or clean sans
- Large product name, small details

## Color
- Studio lighting aesthetic
- Neutral backgrounds (light gray to dark)
- Accent colors from product itself

## Motion
- Smooth orbital rotation
- Scroll-driven camera (dolly, pan, tilt)
- Exploded-view animations
- Material/finish transitions

## Layout
- Product centered, often full-viewport
- Specs in side panels or below
- Scroll sequences changing camera angle

## Texture
- Photorealistic PBR materials
- Environment maps for reflections
- Subtle depth of field

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `PBR materials`, `orbital rotation`, `studio lighting`, `exploded view`, `scroll-driven camera`
**Banned:** `flat product photos`, `2D imagery only`, `busy backgrounds`, `text-heavy layouts`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] 3D product model (Three.js, Spline, or similar)
- [ ] Orbital or scroll-driven camera control
- [ ] Studio lighting (key, fill, rim)
- [ ] PBR materials with reflections
- [ ] Minimal UI around product
- [ ] Neutral background
- [ ] No flat 2D product photos as primary

## When to Use
Consumer electronics, luxury goods, automotive, furniture, footwear, hardware startups, DTC product pages

## Prompts
**Exact:** Build a 3D product showcase with Three.js/React Three Fiber. Photorealistic model with orbital controls, studio lighting, scroll-driven camera. Minimal UI.

**Template:** Build a 3D showcase for [PRODUCT]. Use Three.js or Spline. Studio lighting, orbital/scroll camera, PBR materials. Product-centered layout.
