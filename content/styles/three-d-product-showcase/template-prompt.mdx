## ANTI-SLOP CONSTRAINTS (DO NOT MODIFY)

- NO flat 2D product photos as primary imagery (real-time 3D model is the hero)
- NO 2D-only imagery (a true WebGL three.js/Spline scene must render)
- NO busy backgrounds (neutral studio backdrop — light gray to dark)
- NO text-heavy layouts (minimal labels; product is the focus)
- NO card clutter around the product (minimal chrome, floating spec labels)
- NO harsh top-down lighting (use key / fill / rim kit + soft shadows)
- NO flat opaque colors (PBR materials with metalness, roughness, clearcoat, env reflections)

- NO decorative UI flourishes (grids, rules, ornament) near the object

## FONTS

Typography is quiet — it sits behind the product. Use:
- [DISPLAY_FONT], light weight (200–300): Product name, uppercase, `clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 6rem)`, tracking 0.02em
- Inter 400 (or [DISPLAY_FONT] 400): body captions, 14–16px, max-width 340px
- [MONO_FONT] (JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono): spec labels, 11–12px, uppercase, tracking 0.08em

Load via Google Fonts. Two families maximum: one clean sans for the name, one mono for specs.

## STRUCTURE

### Hero — Full-Viewport Object
- `min-h-[100dvh]`, full-viewport Three.js canvas, product centered, 150%–250% scale
- Orbit drag (auto-drift when idle), scroll = gentle dolly-in
- Bottom-left overlay: product name (huge, light) + one-liner + label `[PRODUCT] / 01`
- CTA: "EXPLORE" — minimal underline chip

### Scroll Camera Sequence (3+ shots)
- Shot 1: dolly-in from 4m to ~1.6m
- Shot 2: tilt/arc to a signature angle (e.g. 45° top-down)
- Shot 3: exploded view with staggered part offsets + rotating parts
- Spec labels float beside each exposed part (mono, uppercase)

### Specs
- Left: persistent mini three.js canvas, slow self-orbit
- Right: spec list in mono, hairline separators, max-width ~280px

### Materials / Finishes (dark stage)
- Swap backdrop to `#0A0A0B`, product close-up
- Swatch list: [MATERIAL_A], [MATERIAL_B], [MATERIAL_C] — PBR values lerp on click

### Footer
- Minimal, one line: `[BRAND] © 2026 — [TAGLINE]`, centered

## LIGHTING KIT (reuse for any product)

- **Key:** area light, 45° above-front, intensity 1.0, warm — dominant soft light
- **Fill:** hemisphere behind-left, 0.08 — lifts shadows without flattening
- **Rim:** tight spot, behind-top-left, ~0.5 — definition against the stage
- **Environment:** HDR env map for reflections (metalness 0.5–1.0, roughness 0.1–0.4, clearcoat on lacquer surfaces)
- **Shadows:** soft contact shadow + blurred shadowMap (PCFSoft); no hard drop shadows

Replace [LIGHT_SETUP] with your tuning: e.g. `low-key spot heavy` for dramatic, `high-key fill heavy` for commercial clean.

---

## CAMERA

- [CAMERA_MOVE]: choose the scroll-driven script —
  - `dolly-in` (push from wide to close-up)
  - `orbit_arc` (lateral 45° arc and settle)
  - `explode` (exploded-view reveal)
  - `flythrough` (interior/for component-level products)
- Idle: auto-orbit at ~0.08 rad/s, paused while dragging.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → no auto-orbit, no scroll dolly, exploded view only on click.

---

## ANIMATIONS

- Product entrance: camera dolly-in 4s, ease-out-cubic
- Scroll camera: values lerped by scroll progress (spring 0.08–0.12)
- Exploded view: parts offset 16px + lift, stagger 60ms, 900ms total
- Material swap: lerp PBR values 500ms ease-out
- Spec labels: fade + 8px rise on viewport enter, 700ms
- Auto-orbit: pause on drag, resume after 1.5s idle

---

## VISUAL RULES (DO NOT MODIFY)

- Real-time 3D model (three.js / R3F / Spline) hero — never a flat photo
- Orbital + scroll-driven camera; interaction is exploration
- Studio lighting rig: key / fill / rim, soft shadows
- PBR materials with environment reflections
- Minimal UI, neutral background
- Quiet typography — huge light product name, tiny mono spec labels
- Accent color comes from the product, not the UI

---

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Product name:** one word, 4–8 letters, uppercase, huge and light. "AURA", "SQUARE", "VERA" — not a sentence.

**One-liner:** 4–8 words, tactile and quiet. "Wireless headphones, sculpted for quiet." not "Our headphones feature advanced noise cancellation."

**Spec labels:** 3–5 uppercase mono details per section. Exact and physical: battery, weight, driver size, codec, material.

**Material names:** 3–6 short swatches (e.g. MATTE, BRUSHED, GLOSS, MILK, NOIR). Each gets a 1-phrase finish descriptor at most.
