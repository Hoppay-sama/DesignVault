# Anti-Slop Checklist

- [ ] Real 3D product model (Three.js, React Three Fiber, Spline, or similar)
- [ ] Orbital or scroll-driven camera control (orbit, dolly, pan, tilt)
- [ ] Deliberate studio lighting — soft key, subtle fill, controlled rim
- [ ] PBR materials with environment-map reflections
- [ ] Product art-directed like a photography shoot (sculptural, not asset-dropped)
- [ ] Interaction feels physical — inertia, damping, and friction on orbit/drag
- [ ] Rotation has purpose — it reveals detail, material, or a feature
- [ ] Neutral studio backdrop (light gray to dark) that catches reflections
- [ ] Minimal UI around the product — specs in side panels or below
- [ ] Large product name, small details; specs in mono or clean sans
- [ ] Exploded-view or material/finish transitions for depth
- [ ] Subtle depth of field and tone mapping (filmic, not raw)
- [ ] Soft shadows — product grounded, contact shadows gradual
- [ ] Performance budget — LOD, capped pixel ratio, compressed textures, 60fps
- [ ] Graceful fallback for low-end devices (static render or simplified scene)
- [ ] Clear affordance — users know they can drag, orbit, or scroll
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (no auto-rotation, no scroll-driven camera)
- [ ] No flat 2D product photos as the primary presentation
- [ ] No default 3D asset look (grey-box materials, placeholder lighting)
- [ ] No gimmicky rotation without purpose
- [ ] No busy backgrounds or text-heavy layouts
- [ ] No harsh lighting without art direction
- [ ] No effects that bury the product (excessive bloom, fog, particles)
- [ ] No unlit or flat-shaded scenes (lighting IS the aesthetic — color 3, texture 5)
