# When to Use

## Perfect For

| Context | Why This Style Works |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Artisan/DTC brands** | Craft-made products feel honest on surfaces with paper tooth and film grain — imperfection signals human hands |
| **Film & photography studios** | The aesthetic literally embodies the medium — analog grain, shutter jitter, uncoated stock |
| **Craft food & beverage** | Roasteries, distilleries, natural-wine and small-batch labels read as small-batch on textured surfaces |
| **Lifestyle/wellness** | Muted warm neutrals and gentle motion feel calm, grounded, and tactile against clinical competitor design |
| **Independent publishers** | Editorial serifs on grainy paper feel like printed periodicals, not web templates |
| **Boutique hotels & hospitality** | A physical-room warmth carried into digital — stone, sand, and clay rooms become muted palettes |
| **Heritage & analog-feel products** | Instruments, journals, film cameras, ceramics — anything where material and touch are the product |

## Brand Personality Match

This style reinforces brands that are:
- **Tactile** — they care about physical material, touch, and surface
- **Warm** — approachable and human, not sterile or clinical
- **Analog** — comfortable with imperfection, jitter, and misregistration
- **Craft-led** — the making is part of the message
- **Quietly confident** — muted, understated, no need for neon

## Avoid When

| Context | Why It Fails |
|---------|-------------|
| **High-volume e-commerce** | The texture-forward, generous-whitespace layout can't carry dense product grids or metrics |
| **Fintech / banking** | Grain and analog noise undermine precision and trust in numbers |
| **SaaS dashboards & analytics** | Data-dense UIs fight the layout — the style is simple, not complex |
| **Tech startups needing "fast" energy** | Gentle 24fps jitter and slow fades feel slow to teams selling speed |
| **Mobile-first utility apps** | Heavy grain overlays blur small type and reduce legibility on small screens |
| **Any brand needing clinical polish** | The banned vocabulary is exactly this style's DNA — no glossy surfaces, no sharp perfection |
| **Content-heavy reference sites** | Long-form docs need clean density; this style is texture-forward and minimal by design |

## How to Decide

Ask: **"Does my brand want to feel like a physical object — printed, warm, tactile, and human rather than digital?"**

If yes → Grain/Noise Texture / Analog Warmth.
If no → Consider Quiet Editorial (if you want restraint without the grain), Organic Botanical Warmth (if you want warm and tactile through nature instead of analog), or Editorial Serif Narrative (if you want editorial type as the hero without texture-forward surfaces).
