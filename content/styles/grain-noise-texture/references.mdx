## Key Visual References

### Texture References
- **Film grain overlays** — fixed, pointer-events:none, opacity 0.03-0.08, never heavy enough to fight text
- **Paper fiber & tooth** — uncoated-stock feel, faint fiber direction, matte not gloss
- **Halftone dots** — risograph misregistration, slight color shift between passes
- **SVG noise** — feTurbulence filters generating the primary surface

### Color References
- **Warm neutrals** — stone #C9C2B4, sand #D9D2C4, clay #B7A99A as the base field
- **Cool mineral tones** — slate #8B9099, graphite #4A4E55, chalk #E3E1DC for contrast sections
- **Reduced-contrast ink** — text is warm off-black/tinted, never pure black on white
- **No neon** — saturation stays muted; accents are low, earthy, desaturated

### Typography References
- **Editorial serif** — high-contrast serif faces for display, read like printed periodicals
- **Warm sans/grotesque** — for modern brands, slightly humanist, not clinical geometric
- **Reduced contrast on text** — ink not pure black, so surfaces feel printed
- **Generous leading** — whitespace lets the grain breathe instead of clutter it

### Motion References
- **Grain jitter** — 24fps subtle: barely perceptible so it never distracts
- **Chromatic shimmer** — occasional noise-seeking offset, slow
- **Slow fades** — 800-1200ms ease-in-out entrances, no snappy eases
- **No bold motion** — the brand's ban on "sharp digital edges" extends to sharp motion

## Screenshot Notes

> When capturing references for this style, look for:
> - The texture (must be film grain / paper / analog surface, not flat digital color)
> - The color palette (must be muted, desaturated — warm or mineral, never neon)
> - The motion (must be gentle, jitter and slow fades — not snappy or bold)
> - The typography (must sit on the texture at reduced contrast, serif or warm sans)
> - The surfaces (must be matte / uncoated — never glossy or clinical)
> - The whitespace (must be generous — texture IS the design element)
> - The overall mood (must feel tactile, warm, analog, human — not perfect)
