# Anti-Slop Checklist

- [ ] Film grain or noise overlay present (SVG feTurbulence or fixed grain, opacity 0.03-0.08)
- [ ] Muted, desaturated palette — warm neutrals (stone, sand, clay) or cool mineral (slate, graphite, chalk)
- [ ] Paper/analog texture — paper fiber, halftone dots, risograph misregistration
- [ ] Gentle motion only — 24fps grain jitter, slow fades
- [ ] Text contrast slightly reduced — ink on stock, not pure black on white
- [ ] Grain is subtle and purposeful — visible on surfaces, invisible in the way
- [ ] Texture layered in a composition — grain + paper + misregistration, not one gimmick
- [ ] Generous whitespace — grain needs room to breathe
- [ ] Layout stays simple — texture is the design element
- [ ] Serif (editorial) or grotesque (modern) type at ease on texture
- [ ] Analog imperfections featured — tooth, jitter, misregistration
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` honored — static grain, no jitter
- [ ] No neon or saturated colors
- [ ] No glossy or clinical surfaces
- [ ] No sharp digital perfection — no crisp machine edges everywhere
- [ ] No heavy-handed Instagram grain filter — no obvious, uniform, plastic noise
- [ ] No muddy legibility — text and texture balanced
- [ ] No fake film look — no cheap scratches/vignette masquerading as analog
