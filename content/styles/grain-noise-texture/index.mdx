# Grain/Noise Texture / Analog Warmth

**Film grain, paper texture, noise overlays. Analog imperfection in digital design. Muted desaturated palettes.**

## Visual DNA
Deliberate reintroduction of analog imperfection. Film grain, paper texture, noise overlays that make screens feel like physical surfaces. Reaction against clinical retina perfection. Muted desaturated palettes that feel printed on uncoated stock.

## Design Principles
1. **TEXTURE IS THE DESIGN** — Grain/noise is primary surface
2. **ANALOG IMPERFECTION** — Misregistration, paper tooth, film jitter
3. **MUTED TO GROUNDED** — Desaturated palettes feel printed

## Typography
- Pairs with both serif (editorial) and grotesque (modern)
- Slightly reduced contrast — text isn't pure black on white

## Color
- Muted, desaturated
- Warm neutrals (stone, sand, clay) or cool mineral tones (slate, graphite, chalk)

## Motion
- Gentle. Grain animates subtly (24fps jitter). Slow fades.

## Layout
- Generous whitespace
- Texture IS the design element — layout stays simple

## Texture
- SVG noise filters (feTurbulence)
- Film grain overlays (fixed, pointer-events:none, opacity 0.03-0.08)
- Paper fiber, halftone dots, risograph misregistration

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `film grain`, `noise overlay`, `paper texture`, `analog`, `tactile warmth`
**Banned:** `clinical perfection`, `neon colors`, `sharp digital edges`, `glossy surfaces`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Film grain or noise overlay present
- [ ] Muted, desaturated palette
- [ ] Paper/analog texture
- [ ] Gentle motion only
- [ ] No neon or saturated colors
- [ ] No glossy or clinical surfaces
- [ ] No sharp digital perfection

## When to Use
Artisan/DTC brands, lifestyle/wellness, craft food & beverage, independent publishers, boutique hotels
