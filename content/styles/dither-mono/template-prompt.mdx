## ANIMATIONS

Use CSS animations with `animation-fill-mode: both`.

| Element | Animation | Duration | Easing | Delay |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|-------|
| Background | Opacity fade-in | 1200ms | `ease-out` | 0ms |
| Headline | Opacity + translateY(20px)→0 | 1000ms | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | 200ms |
| Body text | Opacity fade-in | 800ms | `ease-out` | 400ms |
| Wordmark | Scale(0.95)→1 + opacity | 1400ms | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | 300ms |
| CTA | Border draw-in | 800ms | `ease-in-out` | 800ms |

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → collapse all to 0.01ms.

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** Be specific and concrete. NOT "Build the future of analytics." INSTEAD: "Data Without Compromise" or "Signals, Not Noise."

**Body copy:** Short (max 40 words per block). Specific. No filler words ("seamlessly", "leverage", "empower"). Write like a technical document, not a marketing brochure.

**Data callouts:** Use real or plausible numbers. `ACCURACY: 99.2%`, `SIGNALS: 2.4M/DAY`. Monospace font. These are the "technical marginalia" that signal precision.
