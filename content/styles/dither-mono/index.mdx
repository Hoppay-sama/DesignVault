# Dither Mono

**Brutalist B&W editorial where bitmap texture replaces color as the emotional medium.**

---

## Visual DNA

A world built from black and white only — but never flat. Heavy bitmap dithering creates the illusion of depth and tone where none exists. Photography emerges from darkness like it's being developed in a darkroom. Typography is clean and confident, providing calm contrast to the textured chaos of the imagery. The overall effect is a newspaper printed on obsidian paper — informational, monumental, and slightly dangerous.

The left edge dissolves into pure black. The right edge catches light. In between, dithered photographs tell stories in dots. The brand name doesn't sit in the layout — it overlaps the bottom edge, bleeding off like a wordmark stamped into wet ink. Nothing is colorful. Nothing is friendly. Everything has weight.

## Difficulty

★★★★★ — Requires extreme restraint. Easy to overdo texture. The balance between clean type and processed imagery is unforgiving — too much dither overwhelms, too little loses the style.
