# Vast Quiet Cinematic

**Editorial minimalism meets cinematic scale — vast B&W photography, mist atmosphere, tiny centered sans, extreme whitespace, quiet CTA.**

---

## Visual DNA

Full-bleed desaturated photography or film stills dominate every viewport. Text retreats to small, thin sans-serif at the edges of the frame. The page breathes like a film — slow pacing, long pauses between sections, deliberate reveals. Feels like watching a Terrence Malick film scrolled vertically.

The upper half is pure fog-white sky. The lower half is a desaturated mountain ridge. Between them, a single line of tiny type floats at 12px. There is no CTA shouting. There is a whisper: "Explore" in a thin border. The user doesn't browse — they drift.
