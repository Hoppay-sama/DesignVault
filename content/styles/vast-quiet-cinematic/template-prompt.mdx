## PAGE STRUCTURE

### Hero
- `min-h-[100dvh]`, full-bleed desaturated photograph background
- Text: bottom-left or centered-but-tiny
- Headline: [DISPLAY_FONT] 200, tracking 0.08em, size `var(--headline)` — MAX 2 LINES
- Subtext: [BODY_FONT] 400, max-width 360px, 1-2 sentences
- CTA: thin border, transparent bg, uppercase, tracking 0.15em

### Scene Sections (2-4)
Each section: `min-h-[100dvh]`, massive whitespace
- One full-bleed desaturated photograph per section
- Text: edge-aligned, tiny, wide-tracked
- Body: max 3 sentences per section
- Layout: split (photo + fog-white) OR full-bleed with text overlay

### Footer
- Minimal: brand name, year, tagline
- Monospace or thin sans, tiny size

---

## VISUAL RULES (DO NOT MODIFY)

- Fog-white (#F5F3F0) for text sections
- ALL photography: desaturate 60-80%, reduce contrast, warm grade, film grain (0.04), vignette
- Thin type only (200-300 weight)
- Section gap minimum 6rem
- No cards, no shadows, no borders (except CTA)
- Motion: slow fades (1000ms+), easing `ease-out`

---

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** 1-2 words max. Poetic, not descriptive. "BEYOND THE MAP" not "We Offer Luxury Travel to Remote Destinations."

**Body text:** 1-3 sentences. Evocative, specific. Write like a travel writer, not a marketer.

**Photography:** Use landscape/nature/architecture photography. Desaturate heavily. The image should feel like a film still.
