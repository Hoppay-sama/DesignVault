**Palette guidance:** High-contrast duotone only — black/white or one single color. The accent is a signal, used for one word, one stroke, or one axis state. NO gradients, NO multi-color palettes.

## STRUCTURE

### Hero
- `min-h-[100dvh]`, near-black ground
- Headline: [DISPLAY_FONT], `clamp(4rem, 12vw, 12rem)`, uppercase, tight leading (0.9-0.95)
- Split-text reveal: letter-by-letter stagger on [TRIGGER]
- Scrub: `[SCROLL_SCRUB]` — headline scales from 1 → 1.6 or clips as you scroll

### Marquee Strips (1-2)
- Full-width horizontal marquee, continuous loop (30-60s)
- Uppercase repeated phrase, [DISPLAY_FONT] at 8-12rem
- One strip can run in reverse for counterpoint

### Scrub Section (2-3)
- Each: pinned `min-h-[100dvh]` block, GSAP ScrollTrigger + scrub
- Scroll-driven weight: variable font axis 100→900 mapped to scroll progress
- Word-level choreography: stagger words left-to-right as you pass

### Statement
- One oversized word or phrase, 20-30vw, magnetic follow on cursor
- Ghost-text layer behind: outline-only type, 40% opacity

### Footer
- Ground color, minimal text, one marquee or no motion

## ANIMATIONS

- Split-text reveal: letters/words, stagger 0.05-0.1s, power3.out, triggered on [TRIGGER]
- Scroll-driven weight: variable font `font-variation-settings: 'wght' 100→900`, scrub [SCROLL_SCRUB]
- Horizontal marquee: translateX loop, 30-60s, linear, infinite
- Pinned sequences: GSAP ScrollTrigger `scrub: 1`, `pin: true`
- Magnetic text: cursor proximity, spring-based resistance, 0.3-0.5s
- Scramble/decode: character swap on load or scroll, 800-1200ms per phrase

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → disable all animation, instant reveals, static type.

---

## VISUAL RULES (DO NOT MODIFY)

- Monumental display type (120px+), type IS the hero
- High-contrast duotone palette (B&W or single accent color)
- Variable font weight mapped to scroll (100→900)
- Split-text letter-by-letter or word-by-word reveals
- Continuous horizontal marquees
- GSAP ScrollTrigger pinned text sequences with scrub
- Magnetic cursor-following text
- Tight leading, uppercase, oversized scale contrast
- No imagery unless it serves the type
- No bounce easing — power/expo curves only

---

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** 2-6 words. Short, monumental, declarative. "MOVE THE WHOLE ROOM" not "Our platform enables motion-first communication." Long headlines break the choreography — split into marquee strips instead.

**Body text:** 1-2 sentences max. Sparse. The words you do write must be worth animating.

**Choreography:** One effect per headline. A headline that splits AND scales AND scrambles reads as noise. Choose: letter stagger, or weight scrub, or marquee — not all three on the same line.
