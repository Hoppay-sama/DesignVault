Replace [DISPLAY_FONT] with a heavy grotesk — type with physical presence:

- Signal red #E10600 — protest posters, activism, music

The accent appears ONLY on the single most important statement, active navigation, and one highlight per section. Everything else is ink on paper.

## ANIMATIONS

- Hero display type: clip-path wipe, 400-600ms, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` — arrives fast, stops dead
- Secondary type: hard opacity cut (no fade), 150ms, `steps(1)`
- Body text: opacity fade, 300ms, ease-out
- Micro labels: hard cut, 100ms, `steps(1)`
- Marquee: continuous translateX loop, 20-30s, linear
- Hover states: instant inversions — no transitions, no easing curves

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → all entrance animations collapse to instant, marquee freezes in place.

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** 4-8 words. Direct declarations and commands. "WE ARE NOT YOUR AESTHETIC" not "Our Design Philosophy." No vague aspirational headlines ("Build the future" is banned).

**Body text:** 1-2 sentences. Specific and direct — facts, dates, demands. No marketing filler words.

**Imagery:** Every image must be processed — threshold (pure B&W), halftone dots, posterize (3-4 tonal levels), or duotone (black + accent). Never raw photography.
