# Cinematic Scroll Story

**Multi-layer parallax, scroll-choreographed narrative, rich photography, transparent-edge PNG layers like theater flats.**

## Visual DNA
A sticky viewport containing 7+ parallax layers that shift, scale, blur, and reveal as you scroll 3700px. Scene photographs with transparent edges stack like theater flats. A sight card slider flies in from 420vw on X. Story panels fade in at precise scroll positions. The page is a film you control with your scroll wheel.

## Design Principles
1. **SCROLL IS THE DIRECTOR** — Every pixel of scroll progress triggers precise layer transforms
2. **LAYERS AS THEATER FLATS** — Transparent-edge PNGs stack at different Z-depths with independent parallax
3. **SEGMENT-BASED CHOREOGRAPHY** — Each scroll range is a separate "scene" with its own animation logic

## Typography
- Display: Ogg Medium or editorial serif, clamp(4rem, 14vw, 14rem) for monumental titles
- Body: Inter or clean sans, 16-18px, 1.5 line-height

## Color
- Varies by scene — sky blues, warm creams, deep teals
- Shade gradients for transitions between scenes
- Cream (#fdf1e1) text on dark, ink (#111411) on light

## Motion
- Scroll-driven parallax (lerp smoothing at 0.14)
- segmentInOut transitions per scroll range
- Blur/brightness/saturation shifts between scenes
- Sight card slider fly-in from 420vw with power(1.55) easing
- Infinite slider with 3-set clone + instant-jump normalization

## Layout
- Sticky stage: 100vh, overflow-hidden
- Scroll container: 100vh + 3700px
- 7+ absolute-positioned layers with independent transforms
- Story panels at 45% vertical, centered

## Texture
- Transparent-edge PNG layers (sky, bridge, bazaar, splitframes)
- Shade gradients for scene transitions
- Blur transitions between scenes (14px max)
- Film grain on photographic layers

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `sticky viewport`, `parallax layers`, `transparent-edge PNG`, `scroll-driven choreography`, `segmentInOut`, `lerp smoothing`
**Banned:** `static sections`, `simple fade transitions`, `single-layer photography`, `card-based layout`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Sticky viewport with scroll container
- [ ] 7+ parallax layers with independent transforms
- [ ] Segment-based scroll choreography
- [ ] Transparent-edge PNG layering
- [ ] Lerp smoothing (not raw scroll)
- [ ] Infinite slider with clone+normalize
- [ ] Reduced motion fallback
- [ ] Precise easing (cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1))

## When to Use
Travel/tourism, brand storytelling, documentary sites, cultural institutions, long-form narratives

## Dual Prompts

### Exact Replica
```
Build a cinematic scroll story for "Mostar city". Stack: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (no frameworks).

## STRUCTURE
- cinema-scroll: height calc(100vh + 3700px)
- stage: sticky, 100vh, overflow-hidden
- 7+ layers: sky, back-four, bazaar, splitframe-L/R, bridge, frame-two — all absolute with CSS variable transforms
- JS: lerp smoothing (0.14), segmentInOut for each scene, mouse parallax (0.12)

## SCENES (scroll ranges)
- 0-650px: Title rises -210px, fades out
- 560-1620px: Bridge widens, splits, launches up. Blur ramps to 14px
- 1760-2700px: Bazaar gains saturation, story panel fades in
- 2760-3560px: Sights slider flies in from 420vw (power 1.55)
- 3360-3660px: Nav buttons fade in, become clickable

## ANTI-SLOP
- All images from remote URLs (transparent-edge PNGs)
- Lerp smoothing, not raw scroll
- segmentInOut per scene
- Infinite slider with 3-set clone
- Reduced motion: snap values, disable transitions
```
