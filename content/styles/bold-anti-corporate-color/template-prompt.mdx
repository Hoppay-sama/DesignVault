## ANIMATIONS

- Entrance: scale(0)→1 with overshoot (backOut, 500-700ms, staggered)
- Elastic hover: scale(1)→(1.05-1.06), 250-350ms, backOut
- Wobble loop: rotate ±1.5deg oscillation, 4s, infinite (accent blocks, badges)
- Marquee strip: infinite translateX scroll, 15-25s, linear
- Panel reveals: translateY + rotate into place, springy

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → disable all wobble/marquee/spring, instant entrance.
