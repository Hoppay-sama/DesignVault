# Bold Anti-Corporate Color

**Saturated primaries, collage layout, elastic easing, risograph texture, punk typography. Zine meets punk poster.**

## Visual DNA
Saturated color blocks, hand-drawn elements, typography that breaks every rule. A zine meets a punk poster. Direct rejection of corporate minimalism. Clashing color combinations intentional. Collage-like with intentional misalignment.

## Design Principles
1. **PERSONALITY OVER POLISH** — Imperfection is the point
2. **COLOR AS REBELLION** — Saturated primaries that clash intentionally
3. **COLLAGE COMPOSITION** — Overlapping, misaligned, mixed media

## Typography
- Mixed weights and styles — bold condensed, handwritten, stencil
- All used simultaneously

## Color
- Saturated primaries (hot pink, electric yellow, cobalt blue, lime green)
- Clashing combinations intentional

## Motion
- Bouncy, playful, slightly chaotic
- Wobble, bounce, elastic easing

## Layout
- Asymmetric, overlapping, collage-like
- Intentional misalignment

## Texture
- Risograph, halftone, screen-print texture
- Hand-drawn illustration

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `anti-corporate`, `saturated clash`, `risograph texture`, `collage layout`, `punk typography`
**Banned:** `corporate minimalism`, `muted colors`, `perfect alignment`, `professional polish`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Saturated, clashing colors
- [ ] Collage/overlapping layout
- [ ] Hand-drawn or risograph texture
- [ ] Playful, bouncy motion
- [ ] Intentional misalignment
- [ ] Mixed typography styles
- [ ] No corporate minimalism or muted palettes

## When to Use
Campaign microsites, cultural moments, youth brands, music events, activist organizations
