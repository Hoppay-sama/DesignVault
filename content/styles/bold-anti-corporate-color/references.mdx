## Key Visual References

### Color References
- **Acid yellow** — #D4FF00 (electric, near-neon, the loudest accent in the system)
- **Hot pink** — #FF2D95 (the other dominant saturated accent)
- **Electric orange** — saturated orange at full chroma for the third clash point
- **Cobalt blue** — saturated blue used to complete triadic clashes
- **Lime green** — saturated green when the clash needs a fourth voice
- **Kickers** — near-black, sticker black, or paper cream for collage relief
- **Rule:** clashing combinations are intentional — never muted, never corporate pastel

### Typography References
- **Big bold condensed** — oversized display, tight leading, uppercase shout headlines
- **Handwritten / marker** — hand-drawn annotations, circled words and scribble
- **Stencil / stencil slab** — industrial letterforms that read as stamped or cut
- **All mixed simultaneously** — mixed weights and styles stacked with intent
- **Oversized display type** is the visual center of mass — body is minimal

### Layout References
- **Collage composition** — overlapping layers, imperfect crop, mixed media pasted on
- **Intentional misalignment** — rotated slabs, dropped baselines, off-grid stacks
- **Sticker logic** — elements that look peeled and slapped on top of the base

### Texture References
- **Risograph texture** — visible registration misregistration, paper grain
- **Halftone / screen-print** — dot screen and screen-print bands
- **Hand-drawn marks** — marker highlights, handwritten notes, tear edges

### Motion References
- **Bounce** — elastic easing, overshoot on entry
- **Wobble** — continuous playful jiggle on stickers/badges
- **Slight chaos** — layered entrances and springy overshoot (fast, 400-600ms)

## Screenshot Notes

> When capturing references for this style, look for:
> - The color (must be saturated, clashing primaries — acid yellow, hot pink, cobalt, lime)
> - The layout (must be collage — overlapping, misaligned, never a clean corporate grid)
> - The type (must be oversized, mixed — condensed + handwritten + stencil, not one voice)
> - The texture (must show risograph, halftone or screen-print grain)
> - The motion (must be bouncy, springy, slightly chaotic — not smooth corporate ease)
> - The mood (must feel loud, defiant, punk — never polished or muted)
