# Classical Remix

**White editorial ground, grainy classical figure illustrations, serif italic emphasis, thin orbit lines. Renaissance meets modern SaaS.**

## Visual DNA
White (#FAFAF8) editorial ground with grainy classical figure illustrations — robed scholars, anatomical drawings, celestial maps. Serif italic emphasis words create editorial rhythm. Thin orbit lines continue behind text columns like astronomical diagrams. A blue pill CTA and trust logo row complete the composition.

## Design Principles
1. **CLASSICAL AS AUTHORITY** — Historical illustrations signal depth, craft, permanence
2. **WHITE GROUND, SERIF VOICE** — White space is confident. Serif whispers.
3. **ORBITAL COMPOSITION** — Thin lines connect elements like a scientific diagram

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond 400/600 or Playfair Display, clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)
- Italic emphasis: Same family italic for key phrases
- Body: Clean sans (Inter 400), 15-16px, line-height 1.7

## Color
- Ground: #FAFAF8 (warm white, not pure)
- Ink: #1A1A1A
- Accent: #2563EB (blue pill CTA) or #8B6914 (antique gold)

## Motion
Gentle — illustrations fade in (1000ms), orbit lines draw on via SVG stroke-dasharray (1500ms).

## Layout
- Asymmetric split: text left, classical illustration right
- Orbit lines continue behind text column
- Trust logo row below hero
- Generous margins (max-width 680px body)

## Texture
- Grainy classical illustrations (processed to look aged)
- Thin orbit/celestial lines (0.5px stroke)
- Subtle paper grain (opacity 0.03)

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `classical figure`, `serif italic emphasis`, `white editorial ground`, `orbit lines`, `trust logo row`
**Banned:** `dark backgrounds`, `sans-serif headlines`, `modern photography`, `neon accents`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Warm white ground (#FAFAF8, not #FFF)
- [ ] Serif display font (not sans)
- [ ] Classical/historical illustration (not modern)
- [ ] Orbit or celestial line decoration
- [ ] Trust logo row
- [ ] Blue pill or gold accent CTA
- [ ] Grain on illustrations

## When to Use
Education, luxury wellness, heritage brands, financial services, publishing, premium B2B
