## ANIMATIONS

| Element | Animation | Duration | Easing | Delay |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|-------|

| Orbit lines | SVG stroke draw-in | 1500ms | `ease-in-out` | 300ms |

| Body text | Opacity fade-in | 600ms | `ease-out` | 400ms |
| Trust logos | Staggered opacity fade | 500ms per logo | `ease-out` | 600ms + i*100ms |

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → collapse all to 0.01ms.
