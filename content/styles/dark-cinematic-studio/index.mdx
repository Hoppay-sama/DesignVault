# Dark Cinematic Studio

**Dark moody cinematic with warm cream palette, video backgrounds, serif italic accent, giant pull-up typography.**

## Visual DNA
Dark, moody, cinematic with warm cream (#DEDBC8, #E1E0CC) text on black. Video backgrounds with noise overlays. Serif italic accent text for editorial emphasis. Giant pull-up typography animations. Scroll-linked character opacity reveals. The aesthetic of a filmmaker's studio after midnight.

## Design Principles
1. **WARMTH IN DARKNESS** — Cream text on dark creates intimacy, not intimidation
2. **SERIF ITALIC AS ACCENT** — Single italic serif phrase within clean sans creates editorial punctuation
3. **SCROLL AS NARRATIVE** — Character-by-character opacity reveals tied to scroll create reading rhythm

## Typography
- Display: Almarai or similar clean sans (400-700), clamp(2rem, 5vw, 4rem)
- Italic accent: Instrument Serif italic for key phrases
- Body: Almarai 400, 14-16px

## Color
- Ground: #000000 (pure black — the one style that uses it)
- Text: #DEDBC8 (warm cream), #E1E0CC (slightly lighter cream)
- Accent: warm amber or cream tones only

## Motion
- Pull-up text reveals (staggered 0.08s delay, y:20→0)
- Scroll-linked character opacity (0.2→1 based on scroll position)
- Card scale+fade entrances (cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1))

## Layout
- Full viewport sections
- Video backgrounds with noise overlay
- Left-aligned content with generous margins
- 4-column card grid for features

## Texture
- SVG noise overlay (feTurbulence, opacity 0.7, mix-blend-mode: overlay)
- Video backgrounds (dark, ambient, slow)
- Warm gradient overlays on video

## Vocabulary
**Primary:** `warm cream on black`, `serif italic accent`, `pull-up text animation`, `scroll-linked character reveal`, `noise overlay`
**Banned:** `cold blue tones`, `bright colors`, `static images only`, `sans-serif everything`

## Anti-Slop
- [ ] Warm cream text (#DEDBC8), not white
- [ ] Video background with noise overlay
- [ ] Serif italic for accent phrases
- [ ] Pull-up or staggered text animations
- [ ] Scroll-linked character reveals
- [ ] Dark ground throughout
- [ ] No cold blue or purple tones

## When to Use
Creative studios, filmmaker portfolios, production houses, premium creative tools, agencies
