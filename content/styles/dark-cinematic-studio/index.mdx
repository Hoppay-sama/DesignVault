# Dark Cinematic Studio

**Dark moody cinematic with warm cream palette, video backgrounds, serif italic accent, giant pull-up typography.**

---

## Visual DNA

Dark, moody, cinematic — the aesthetic of a filmmaker's studio after midnight. Warm cream (#DEDBC8, #E1E0CC) text on pure black creates intimacy, not intimidation. Video backgrounds with noise overlays add the texture of film grain. Serif italic accent text provides editorial punctuation within clean sans body. Giant pull-up typography animations create drama. Scroll-linked character opacity reveals make reading a cinematic experience.

This is the ONE style that uses pure black (#000000) — because the video backgrounds need it. The pure black creates the "screen in a dark room" feeling that video backgrounds require. The warm cream text prevents the black from feeling cold. The noise overlay adds the analog warmth of actual film. Every element feels like it was shot on a camera, not designed in Figma.

## Difficulty

★★★★☆ — Requires motion sensitivity. The pull-up animations and scroll-linked reveals must feel cinematic, not gimmicky. The serif italic accent must be placed surgically. The video backgrounds must be dark and ambient, not bright and distracting.
