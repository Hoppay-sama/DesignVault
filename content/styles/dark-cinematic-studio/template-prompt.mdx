## ANIMATIONS

| Element | Animation | Duration | Easing | Delay |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|-------|
| Video background | Opacity fade-in | 2000ms | `ease-out` | 0ms |
| Giant display text | Pull-up (translateY 100%→0) + opacity | 1200ms | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | 300ms + i*80ms |
| Body text | Scroll-linked character opacity (0.2→1.0) | Tied to scroll | — | — |
| Cards | Scale(0.95)→1 + opacity | 800ms | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | 400ms + i*100ms |
| CTA | Opacity + scale(0.9)→1 | 600ms | `ease-out` | 800ms |

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → video pauses, all motion collapses to 0.01ms, text at full opacity.
