## ANIMATIONS

| Element | Animation | Duration | Easing | Repeat |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|--------|
| Cloud layers | Horizontal drift (translateX) | 30-60s | `linear` | Infinite |
| Binary characters | Opacity shimmer | 4-8s (random) | `ease-in-out` | Infinite |
| Data stream lines | Vertical scroll | 20-40s | `linear` | Infinite |
| Headline | Opacity + translateY(16px)→0 | 1000ms | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | Once |
| CTA | Opacity + glow fade-in | 800ms | `ease-out` | Once |

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → stop all ambient animations, collapse entrances to 0.01ms.

## CONTENT GUIDANCE

**Headlines:** Be specific and data-forward. NOT "Build the future of monitoring." INSTEAD: "Signals in the noise" or "Data, not dashboards."

**Body copy:** Short (max 40 words per block). Specific. Technical register. No filler words. Write like a product spec, not a marketing brochure.

**Data callouts:** Use real or plausible numbers with units. `NODES: 12,847`, `PACKETS/SEC: 2.4M`, `LATENCY: 12ms`. Monospace font. These are the "technical anchors" in the atmosphere.
