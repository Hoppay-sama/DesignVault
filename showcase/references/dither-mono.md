# References - Dither Mono

Source content: `content/styles/dither-mono/references.mdx`
Live-site teardowns below add to it. Each entry: mechanic / stack / what to steal.

## Teardowns

### Nothing (nothing.tech)
- **Mechanic:** Product pages as dot-matrix systems - glyph grids, monochrome surfaces, technical micro-labels, the phone as a printed object.
- **Steal conceptually:** A systematic glyph/grid language can carry a whole brand. Mono is not a limitation if the *pattern* is the identity.

### Playdate - Panic (play.date)
- **Mechanic:** 1-bit handheld console brand site; black/white with exactly one warm accent (yellow), dot-matrix type, playful-industrial tone.
- **Steal conceptually:** The single-accent rule done strictly - the accent only appears where action or emphasis exists. Hardware as editorial object.

### Teenage Engineering (teenage.engineering)
- **Mechanic:** Industrial product catalog; monumental product imagery, tiny mono spec labels, obsessive alignment, near-black ground.
- **Steal conceptually:** Spec-sheet typography (tiny mono) against monumental objects creates the "processed, informational, slightly dangerous" register. Alignment is the aesthetic.

### Noomo Agency (noomoagency.com)
- **Mechanic:** Raw typographic statements, broken grid, high-contrast B&W with intentional roughness.
- **Steal conceptually:** Break the grid *deliberately* - one element per section may violate alignment; everything else stays strict.

### Kestrel v4 (in-repo concept, `referrence/prompts/example_prompt7.md`)
- **Mechanic:** Monumental wordmark bleeding off the bottom edge; dithered subject emerging from darkness; left edge dissolving to black; single amber accent.
- **Steal conceptually:** Light as a compositional force - the subject emerges from black rather than sitting on a background.

## Technique references

- Floyd-Steinberg dither on portraits (organic dot pattern, tonal range)
- Ordered/Bayer dither on technical imagery (regular grid)
- 1-bit threshold on icons (maximum contrast)

## Motion vocabulary

staggered reveal, mask/clip reveal, hard cut (no crossfade), micro-label tick,
wordmark bleed, rise (power ease, no bounce), reduced-motion collapse.

## Capture checklist

Dither pattern quality (FS vs ordered vs halftone), monumental-to-body type ratio,
wordmark treatment (bleed vs static), near-black ground (not pure black), where the
single accent appears, zero raw photography.
