# References - Vast Quiet Cinematic

Source content: `content/styles/vast-quiet-cinematic/references.mdx`
Live-site teardowns below add to it. Each entry: mechanic / stack / what to steal.

## Teardowns

### Kononenko Architectural Bureau - Reksa Andhika (Awwwards SOTD)
- **Mechanic:** Architecture portfolio as a quiet, full-bleed film; space between projects does the talking.
- **Steal conceptually:** Absence as composition - one image per viewport, nothing competing. The pause is the design.

### ERA Residence - The First The Last (Awwwards SOTD)
- **Mechanic:** Residential architecture story; cinematic imagery, minimal type, patient pacing.
- **Steal conceptually:** Scroll speed communicates permanence. Slow reveals make still images feel filmic.

### Oimachi (Awwwards SOTD)
- **Mechanic:** Place-as-atmosphere; typography at edges, imagery as environment.
- **Steal conceptually:** Anchor UI to viewport edges - corners and margins - and leave the center to the image.

### RISK - FLOT NOIR (risk.film)
- **Mechanic:** Full-bleed cinematic photography, tiny edge-aligned type, extreme whitespace, slow crossfades.
- **Steal conceptually:** Type behaves like film credits: small, tracked, present but never loud.

### Julien Calot - FLOT NOIR (juliencalot.com)
- **Mechanic:** Desaturated landscapes, thin sans at viewport edges, quiet CTA, film grain overlay.
- **Steal conceptually:** Grain and desaturation unify disparate photography into one world. The CTA is a whisper, not a button.

### 21 Hrs On The Moon - Studio 28K (21hrs.space)
- **Mechanic:** Vast photography, minimal text, slow reveals, atmospheric pacing.
- **Steal conceptually:** A few images taken seriously beat many images shown quickly. Sound design as atmosphere.

### Apple AirPods Pro
- **Mechanic:** Scroll-driven product film with immaculate restraint; every frame composed.
- **Steal conceptually:** Choreograph the *camera*, not the elements - one continuous move per section.

## Motion vocabulary

crossfade / dissolve, slow reveal, fade-in-place, parallax drift (subtle),
film grain, letterboxing, reduced-motion fallback (static frames, no drift).

## Capture checklist

Image-to-text ratio (80%+ image), type weight (200-300, never 400+), excessive
whitespace between sections, desaturated graded photography, quiet CTA treatment,
overall feeling - a film, not a website.
